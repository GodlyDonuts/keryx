# Privacy

Keryx is designed not to need personal data.

- The index is built from public job postings and public source repositories.
- The dashboard has no account system, analytics, cookies, advertisements, remote fonts, or third-party
  scripts.
- Saved roles are identifiers stored only in the visitor's browser using `localStorage`. They are never
  transmitted to Keryx. Clearing site data removes them.
- Search terms and filters are evaluated in the browser and are not sent to Keryx.
- Keryx does not accept applications, upload resumes, collect email addresses, or profile applicants.

Opening an employer application link leaves Keryx. The employer and its recruiting platform then
control their own data practices, which applicants should review before submitting information.
